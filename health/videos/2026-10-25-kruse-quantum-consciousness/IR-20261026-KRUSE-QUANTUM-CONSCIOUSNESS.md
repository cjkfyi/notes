# Quantum Biology and the Unfalsifiable Framework

**Source** — "Dr. Jack Kruse Explains Physics of Consciousness", `@FitnessWisdomPodcast`, ~1:23.
Transcript: `transcript.md`.

> **Not medical advice.** Several claims here are contradicted by published evidence, and one — that
> creatine can kill non-athletes — is refuted by a large safety literature. Do not act on this source.

A long interview in which a neurosurgeon presents a personal framework he calls the "decentralized
thesis," and a sympathetic host amplifies it. The format is promotional rather than journalistic: the
host offers no challenge, no request for evidence, and no alternative explanation, and closes by saying
"we need to spread your word."

The problem is not that the claims are uniformly wrong. It is that they are **layered**, and the
layering is a credibility-laundering device.

## The four layers

A reader's natural strategy is to check what can be checked. Layer 1 is checkable and correct, so the
reader concludes the source is reliable and stops.

| Layer | Definition | Test |
|---|---|---|
| **1 — Established** | Correct, textbook, checkable | Verify against a reference |
| **2 — Contested** | A real research programme with a disputed interpretation | Ask whether consensus exists |
| **3 — Unsupported** | An inference that does not follow, or an extraordinary claim without evidence | Ask what would support it |
| **4 — False / harmful** | Contradicted by evidence, or a claim with a harm pathway | Ask whether it is refuted |

## Layer 1: what is correct

Oxygen is the second most electronegative element after fluorine (3.44 vs 3.98, Pauling). Molecular oxygen
is paramagnetic with a triplet ground state and accessible singlet states. CO₂ is diamagnetic. The inner
mitochondrial membrane maintains ~150–180 mV across ~5 nm. Cristae geometry is functionally significant
and coupled to respiratory efficiency. Light entrains circadian biology through the retinohypothalamic
tract to the suprachiasmatic nucleus, with melanopsin-mediated blue light suppressing melatonin.
Cytochromes are iron-sulfur proteins and Complex IV binds copper. Melanin is paramagnetic with
semiconductor-like behaviour. Ultraweak photon emission from living tissue is real and measurable. Marcus
theory, Landauer's principle and Prigogine's dissipative structures are all real.

**L1-6 is the strongest thing in the interview**, and it is the one that carries the practical advice.

The step from "biophotons exist" to "consciousness is biophoton retention" is not small. Emission is on
the order of tens to hundreds of photons per cm² per second — an extraordinarily low flux, with no
demonstrated receiver, no demonstrated encoding, and no demonstrated causal role. **Existence is not
function**, and the source treats the first as the second throughout.

## The lost unit

The most instructive item is not a wrong number. The source refers to "that 30 million volt charge that
Nick Lane has talked about" across the inner membrane. The potential is ~150–180 mV; across a ~5 nm
membrane that is a field of

```
0.150 V / 5 × 10⁻⁹ m = 3.0 × 10⁷ V/m = 30 MV/m
```

**The figure is exactly right and the unit has been dropped.** "30 million volts" is impossible by eight
orders of magnitude; "30 million volts per metre" is correct. Real quantities, correct magnitudes,
missing dimensions — the characteristic failure of the whole source.

## Layer 2: contested, presented as settled

**EZ water and coherent domains.** The source says the dielectric constant of water "goes from 160 at its
best down to 78." The 78.4 is bulk liquid water at 25 °C and is standard. The ~160 is the value claimed
for the interfacial exclusion-zone phase in Pollack's work and in Del Giudice–Preparata coherent-domain
theory — measured at an interface, not in bulk, and the interpretation is contested.

Presenting the two as one continuum is a category error: they are measured in different geometries, and
no measurement supports a tissue-level dielectric constant that varies between them. Pollack's
exclusion-zone work is real and interesting, and Del Giudice and Preparata were serious physicists. The
existence of an ordered interfacial layer is not fringe. The claim that it is the body's light-storage
medium at a dielectric constant of 160, controlling consciousness, is.

**Biophoton signalling.** Existence solid; signalling function not established. The field's own summary
position is that emission is a byproduct of oxidative metabolism with a plausible oxidative-stress
correlation. The "5,000 times more light from prokaryotes" figure attributed to Popp could not be located
and should not be repeated.

**Deuterium-depleted water.** A real product with weak evidence, promoted to a master variable. DDW has
been studied as a cancer adjunct, principally by one Hungarian group, in small trials with methodological
limitations. Nothing supports "insomnia is deuterium." Its function in the framework is to be the
unmeasurable variable that explains individual variation.

## Layer 3: unsupported leaps

| Claim | Why unsupported |
|---|---|
| Consciousness is the retention of metabolically generated light | No mechanism, measurement or receiver. The hard problem is *redefined* into one the framework can solve, then answered |
| **Copper-65 specifically** (not Cu-63) is required at Complex IV to make DDW | Extraordinary isotope-specific biochemistry, no citation. Both are stable at 69%/31% natural abundance |
| Sleep requires a magnetic polarity shift | Inferred from an anecdote about salamanders on a magnet, generalised to human sleep architecture |
| Tumour grade is determined by vortex geometry via Euler's equation | Would be a major oncological result; offered as a prediction for "50 or 60 years down the road," which is unfalsifiable by construction |
| Collective consciousness is quantum entanglement | Entanglement does not survive decoherence at body temperature; "the GPS system in the brain" is not a measurable entity |
| Cancer is a cell survival mechanism seeking a latitude change | Teleological, no mechanism, and inverts the standard account of clonal selection |
| Astronauts to Mars "will all get cancers almost right away" | A specific empirical prediction with no evidence |

The common form: **a real quantity from physics is imported into biology, given a functional role, and
asserted.** Marcus theory, Landauer's principle, Euler's equation, the Zeeman effect, the Larmor radius,
the Bloch sphere, the brachistochrone — all real, none established as operative in the way claimed. The
vocabulary does the work that evidence would otherwise have to do.

The most important of these is the first, because it is the thesis. Note the structure: the hard problem
is defined as "how do we store light?", then answered by "we store it mostly in water." The problem has
been substituted, not solved.

## Layer 4: false and harmful

**The creatine claim.** "If you give creatine to somebody who's not an elite athlete, it can kill them…
it could actually give you cancer. It actually could cause clots."

This is false. Creatine monohydrate has an extensive safety literature, no evidence of carcinogenicity,
no plausible thrombotic mechanism at supplemental doses, and E1 evidence for strength and lean mass. The
claim is actionable, specific, and points away from a safe and effective intervention — the only
statement in the source with a clear path from belief to harm.

The structural irony: it is the framework's **only falsifiable prediction**, which is why it is also its
only refuted one. The theory becomes testable exactly where it becomes wrong.

**The named-individual claims.** An NFL quarterback's bilateral pulmonary embolism is attributed to blood
"viscosity" from a circumpolar flight; an actress's death is attributed to the same mechanism. Neither
patient's records are available to the speaker, no data are offered, and both events have well-understood
differential diagnoses that do not require the framework. Attributing a real person's death to an invented
mechanism, without evidence, in a monetised video is not a theoretical error.

## Why it cannot be wrong

The framework's characteristic move turns any observation into confirmation:

```
Observation O  →  mechanism M  →  O confirms M
Not-O          →  different mechanism M'  →  still confirms the framework
```

A patient with brain fog on exercise "has a deficit in their consciousness." A patient without it has a
different position on the spectrum. And the spectrum defence is stated explicitly: *"everybody assumes
that every human is the same… this system is all on a continuum. It's on a spectrum and the spectrum
isn't just one spectrum. It's multiple."*

And at 59:06, asked what the break point is: *"I don't think there is a break point… it's a spectrum."*

**A multi-dimensional, unbounded spectrum with no measured axes can accommodate any outcome.** The
framework declares in advance that it has no threshold, so it cannot be tested, and its advice therefore
cannot be sorted into what works and what does not.

The authority structure compounds this. There is no external literature cited for any layer-3 claim.
Where external work is named — Popp, Becker, Pollack, Del Giudice — it establishes the *possibility* of
the mechanism; the step from possibility to the specific claim is made by assertion. The citation pattern
is: real scientists establish that something is conceivable, the speaker establishes that it is true.

## The commercial structure

Five sponsor reads in one episode:

| Sponsor | Product | Mechanism it addresses |
|---|---|---|
| Circadian glasses | Three-lens blue-light filtering | The light-environment thesis |
| Light panel | 10-wavelength panel | The "indoor light is deficient" thesis |
| Shilajit | Mineral supplement | The "mitochondrial minerals" thesis |
| HRV device | Morning measurement | The "nervous-system state" thesis |
| Vagus stimulator | Electrical vagal stimulation | The "parasympathetic shift" thesis |

Each is a purchase the preceding argument recommends. The interview does not merely happen to have
aligned sponsors; **the thesis is a demand-generation mechanism for the ad inventory.** That does not make
the claims false, but the format has no incentive to test them, and the audience is being sold the
solution to a problem the episode defines.

Every device substitutes for evidence rather than supplementing it.

## What is safe

Stated plainly, because a reader may otherwise discard the useful part: get daylight, especially in the
morning; dim or eliminate artificial light in the evening; sleep in a dark room; do not train hard when
acutely unwell; do not take long-haul flights while acutely ill; reduce unnecessary screen exposure at
night. All correct, all endorsed by mainstream practice, **none of which requires the theory.**

Three items should be flagged for a reader who has adopted the framework: do not decline creatine on this
basis; do not substitute relocation for oncology; do not extend the "do not eat on flights" rule to
illness generally — caloric restriction during acute illness or cancer treatment is a clinical decision.

## Verification

Pollack's exclusion-zone work was characterised from secondary knowledge, not primary papers. Popp's
emission figures were not verified. The DDW trial record was not reviewed. The creatine safety claim rests
on the established literature and is the highest-priority item to cite. The framework's own primary
sources — a Patreon blog and an unpublished thesis — were not obtained, which is itself part of the
finding: the framework cannot be assessed without them.
