# bio — video source archive

Notes vault for the biology of cognition and development: bioelectric patterning, basal cognition,
morphogenesis, regeneration, and the "diverse intelligence" programme.

Fourth topic area. Same layout and conventions as `../cybernet/`, `../health/`, `../neuroai/`.

## Why a separate area

`neuroai/` asks how neurons and neuromorphic hardware compute. This asks a prior question: **what
counts as a cognitive system at all**, and what does it mean for a tissue with no brain to solve a
problem. The two overlap on credit assignment and share a literature, but the evidence standards
differ in a specific way — see below.

## The convention that matters here: split grading

Sources in this area frequently mix **published results**, **pre-publication claims**, and
**metaphysical framework** in a single breath, with no change in tone. A single reliability grade
would therefore be misleading. Every report here grades by claim class:

| Class | Grade basis |
|---|---|
| **Empirical** — testable against data | Named lab, methods, prior record, specific quantities |
| **Framework** — organising hypothesis | Presented as hypothesis; not yet testable |
| **Metaphysical** — not decidable as stated | Flagged as such; assessed as a claim, never endorsed |
| **Methodological** — about how to investigate | Assessed for soundness of the argument |

And every claim carries its **stated publication status** in a ledger, because the most quotable
sentences in this genre are usually the unpublished ones.

Other conventions specific to this area:

- **Distinguish redescription from explanation.** A framework that is satisfied by every possible
  observation is not an empirical hypothesis, however interesting it is. Test: what does it predict
  that the observation does not already contain?
- **Check for theorem collisions.** "Free lunches," "random systems are already optimal," and similar
  claims need to be squared with No Free Lunch and its scope before they are repeated.
- **Watch the metric.** Composite measures (Φ, causal emergence, integration) are frequently
  contested and are presented as measurements. Name the contest.
- **Observation is not intervention.** A system that always does X and a system trying to do X look
  identical until you obstruct one.

## Open threads

- **The barium paper.** The empirical anchor of the whole programme and the strongest single result in
  the archive: a tissue solving a novel toxin problem with a minimal transcriptional response. Must be
  located and read before §4.1 is relied on.
- **The ratchet is in review.** "Forced forgetting preserves causal-emergence gains" is the
  load-bearing claim for the archive's refined model (§7.3). Unverified.
- **No Free Lunch has no answer in the source.** A critic reaches for it immediately. The
  structured-distribution reply in the report is the analyst's construction and needs checking.
- **Causal emergence is contested.** The finding may survive a less contested integration measure;
  the report suggests one, but has not tested it.
- **"Semantic medicine" needs a definition.** As stated it is a research-allocation claim, not a
  paradigm. The MAMbot platform is the closest thing to an operationalization.
- **Part one of the interview** is not in the archive; the planarian canon is assumed rather than
  explained.
